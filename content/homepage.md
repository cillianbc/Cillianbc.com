## The Services

### SEO That Holds Up as AI Changes the Rules
**Traditional SEO** — technical foundations, content strategy, on-page structure — remains the base layer of everything. The difference is that the standards AI systems use to evaluate trustworthiness and authority are stricter than what Google's algorithm tolerated five years ago. An SEO audit now needs to consider both traditional ranking and AI citability.

### GEO — Structured to Be Cited
**Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)** is the practice of structuring content so that AI-powered search engines — Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini — retrieve and cite it when generating answers. GEO is not a replacement for SEO. It requires its own approach: definition blocks, extractable paragraphs, semantic markup, and entity consistency across your digital presence.

### AI Workflows — Less Busywork, More Output
**AI workflow design** means building practical systems — using tools like Claude, GPT-4, and Gemini — that reduce the time your team spends on repeatable content tasks. Not replacing your team. Removing the friction that stops them doing the work that matters.
